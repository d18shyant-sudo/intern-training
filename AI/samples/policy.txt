Expense Policy

Meal allowance:
Employees can claim up to ₹2500 per day.

Hotel allowance:
Employees can claim up to ₹5000 per night.

Taxi allowance:
Employees can claim up to ₹500 per day.

Food expenses require the original receipt.

Travel claims must be submitted within 30 days.

Claims above ₹10000 require manager approval.

Only business-related expenses are reimbursable.
